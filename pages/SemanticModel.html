The Semantic Model

Semantic means meaningful. The model turns separate tables into a connected universe that speaks business language.

Real-world analogy

From tables to a data universe

Nothing in real life is a table. Things are connected, and a good model brings those connections back. Click any table to see what it connects to.

  1. Flat tables. Separate sheets, and meaning lost between them.
  2. Star. One fact table at the centre, with dimensions around it.
  3. Snowflake. A dimension branches into another table.
  4. Star cluster. Some dimensions stay flat and some branch, depending on need.
  5. Galaxy. Several fact tables share the same dimensions.
Explain more

Microsoft renamed Power BI “datasets” to “semantic models” in 2023. Galaxy schemas are also called fact constellations. Star schemas are usually the best fit for Power BI; snowflake is the term you’ll see most often for branched dimensions, while “star cluster” is rarer.
