📏 The measure sees 100 of 100 rows Active rows: 100%. Rows are never deleted, only hidden.
  • Row the measure can see
  • Hidden row: dots show which filters hide it
  • Dimension member kept by its filter
  • White bar on a customer = its sales still visible (sent back by the gold two-way stream)
  • Customer with no visible sales: filtered out by the two-way relationship

What flows is the filter, not the data. The Sales fact table always holds all 100 rows. When you drag a dimension's slider, a pulse in that dimension's colour travels down its relationship; when it reaches the fact table, every row that doesn't match is hidden from the measure (dimmed, with a dot in that dimension's colour). A row hidden by two filters carries two dots, because filters intersect: the measure sees only rows that pass every filter. Most relationships are single-direction. Customer is bidirectional: after the other filters act on the fact table, a pulse travels back and dims the customers who no longer have any visible sales (dashed chips). That's how “which customers bought this product?” works. It's the same idea as the Filter Context Camera: the data stays, the frame shrinks.
