Real-world analogy

The hourglass, read from the bottom up

  1. Fact. An endless ocean of what is.
  2. Data. We observe and collect the relevant facts.
  3. Information. We extract the juice. Noise becomes meaning.
  4. Knowledge. We explore it in depth. This looks at the past.
  5. Intelligence. We apply it and choose. This acts in the present.
  6. Wisdom. Insight and foresight. This looks to the future.
