Working with AI

Asking AI Well

AI is getting better at how every month. Knowing what to do, what to ask and whether the answer is right remains your job. That is why this path teaches concepts first.

Never paste real client data into a public AI tool. PAN, GSTIN, bank details and client financials stay out. Share column names and a few made-up rows, or use synthetic data, as H Mart does. Check your firm’s policy and your duty of confidentiality first.

What every good prompt contains

  1. The business question, in plain words, not the formula you think you need.
  2. The structure, with exact table and column names and how the tables relate.
  3. The rules, such as an April to March year or which date counts as the sale date.
  4. A way to check, by asking for one figure you can verify yourself.

Prompt templates

Check every answer

  1. Reconcile. Does the total match the source or the trial balance?
  2. Test the total row. In the H Mart case study, the AI-built target measure read 926% at the total and looked fine everywhere else.
  3. Try a filter you can verify by hand. One month, one customer.
  4. Ask it to explain, then see if you agree. If you cannot explain a formula to your manager, do not ship it.
  5. Be alert with law and standards. AI can invent sections and rates. Verify them at the source, and review anything that will be filed or relied upon.

Proof that this works

The H Mart FMCG case study was vibe-coded from end to end, from the synthetic data to the finished report. The human part was deciding what to build and catching what was wrong.
