Drilling Down

Drilling Down moves from a summary to finer detail, one level at a time: Year → Quarter → Month in Time, or Region → Street → Address in Place. Click any cell of the cube, then click a quarter (or street) to go one level deeper. Drag any cube to rotate it; double-click to reset the view. ⬇️

Rolling Up

Rolling Up is the reverse of drilling: detail is aggregated into broader groups. Watch small Month cubes merge into Quarter cubes, then into one Year cube, with each level staying visible (or Addresses → Streets → Regions). The Total column never changes, because rolling up only regroups the same sales. Drag the blocks to rotate; double-click to reset. ⬆️
