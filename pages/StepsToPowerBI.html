Real-world analogy

The assembly line

📦
  1. Ask. Only a person can frame the question.
  2. Prepare and Process. Power Query does the ETL.
  3. Analyze. The data model and DAX find the answer.
  4. Share. Reports and the Power BI service deliver it.
  5. Act. A person decides, and the question starts again.
