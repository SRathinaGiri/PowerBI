Kinds of Data Analytics

Each kind answers a different question. A visit to the doctor uses nearly all of them.

Real-world analogy

A visit to the doctor

See it through
  1. Descriptive. What happened?
  2. Diagnostic. Why did it happen?
  3. Predictive. What will happen?
  4. Prescriptive. What should I do?
  5. Exploratory. What is interesting?
  6. Confirmatory. Is it really true?
  7. Cognitive. What is right here?
Explain more

The first four are the standard ladder of analytics. Google Maps suggesting a route and YouTube suggesting a video are everyday prescriptive examples. Exploratory and confirmatory describe how you approach data: look for patterns first, then test them. Cognitive analytics is an AI-assisted extension rather than one of the standard four.
