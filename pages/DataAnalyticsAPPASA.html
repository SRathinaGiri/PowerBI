Data Analytics: APPASA

Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, Act: the six steps from the Google Data Analytics certificate.

Real-world analogy

Cooking a meal for guests

A good analysis runs like a good meal: know what is wanted before you touch an ingredient, and don’t stop at serving.

  1. Ask. What do we need to know?
  2. Prepare. Gather the data.
  3. Process. Clean it and make it usable.
  4. Analyze. Find the answer in it.
  5. Share. Present it so others understand.
  6. Act. Decide, do it, and learn from the result.
